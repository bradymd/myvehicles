# MyVehicles

An emergency-first, offline-first vehicle manager — your **car wallet** for breakdown, accident, and police stop situations.

Keep all your vehicle details, insurance, breakdown cover, MOT history, service records, and driver profiles in one place, ready when you need them most.

## Features

**Emergency Screens**
- Breakdown — instant access to your breakdown provider, membership number, and what to do
- Accident — step-by-step guide with insurance details and emergency contacts
- Police Stop — licence, insurance, and vehicle details at your fingertips

**Vehicle Management**
- Multiple vehicles with full specs (registration, VIN, engine, colour, etc.)
- Purchase details and finance tracking (owned, PCP, HP, lease)
- Insurance policy details and renewal dates
- Breakdown and recovery provider info

**MOT & Service Tracking**
- MOT history with advisories and expiry dates
- Service history with costs, mileage, and garage details
- Next service reminders

**Multi-Driver Profiles**
- Driver licence details and categories
- Emergency contact and blood type for each driver
- Licence photo storage (front and back)

**Document Management**
- Attach photos and PDFs to vehicles or drivers
- OCR text recognition from document photos

**Backup & Restore**
- Full backup to ZIP file
- Automatic backups
- Restore from any backup
- Data integrity checking

## Privacy

MyVehicles stores all data locally on your device. No accounts, no cloud sync, no tracking. The only network request is fetching fonts from Google Fonts.
